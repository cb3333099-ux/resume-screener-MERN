# Sample Job Descriptions

## 1) Full Stack MERN Developer

We are looking for a Full Stack MERN Developer with 3+ years of experience building web applications.

Required skills:
- JavaScript, TypeScript, React, Node.js, Express, MongoDB
- REST API development
- Git and Agile workflows

Preferred / Nice to have:
- AWS, Docker, Kubernetes
- Jest or Cypress testing

Education: Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or related field.

---

## 2) Data Analyst (Python)

Minimum 2 years of experience in analytics roles.

Must have:
- Python, SQL, Pandas, NumPy
- Data visualization and dashboarding
- Communication and stakeholder collaboration

Preferred qualifications:
- Machine learning basics, scikit-learn
- Cloud exposure (GCP or AWS)

Education: Bachelor's required, Master's preferred.

---

## 3) Senior Backend Engineer

At least 5 years of backend development experience with distributed systems.

Requirements:
- Node.js or Go
- PostgreSQL and Redis
- Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD (GitHub Actions or Jenkins)
- Strong API design skills (REST/GraphQL)

Nice to have:
- Terraform, AWS
- Mentorship experience

Education: Bachelor's or Master's in Computer Science.
